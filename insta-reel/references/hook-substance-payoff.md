# Instagram Hook → Substance → Payoff Framework

Use this after research is complete. Transform raw facts into scroll-stopping Reel structure.

## 1. Hook (first 1–3 seconds)

Goal: Stop the scroll. Create instant emotional recognition or curiosity gap.

Patterns that work for SkillSarmaya-style finance content:
- Personal regret: “Remember the night you had to ask someone for money…”
- Direct command: “Open your bank statement right now.”
- Shocking contrast: “You have 3 lakh sitting in a committee… and you still can’t touch one rupee.”
- Time travel: “You, ten years ago…”

Rules:
- Max 12–15 words spoken / 2 short lines on screen.
- One strong visual that matches the emotion.
- Yellow highlight on the most painful or surprising word.

## 2. Substance (middle 60–70% of runtime)

Structure inside Substance:
1. Mini-story or concrete example (the person / the situation).
2. Data or visual proof (chart, list, calculation, timeline).
3. Insight / reframe (“List One is looking rich. List Two is being wealthy.”).

Keep scenes short (3–6 seconds each). Alternate illustrated story scenes with clean data scenes.

## 3. Payoff (last 4–7 seconds)

- One clear, memorable action or principle.
- Soft CTA that feels like advice from a friend, not a sales pitch.
- Optional series teaser if this is part of a series (“Part 2 drops tomorrow”).

## Humanizer Pass (mandatory)

After drafting Hook/Substance/Payoff copy:
- Remove every corporate or AI-sounding phrase.
- Prefer short sentences, contractions, rhetorical questions.
- Speak like a sharp older cousin who actually manages money well.
- Run a second pass to cut any line longer than 14 words.

Output format for the copy stage:
```markdown
## Hook
[spoken line]
[on-screen text lines]

## Substance Scenes
### Scene 1
...
### Scene N
...

## Payoff
[spoken + on-screen]
```
