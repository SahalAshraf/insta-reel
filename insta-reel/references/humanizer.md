# Humanizer Pass

Run this on every piece of spoken or on-screen copy before it is locked.

## Checklist
- [ ] No sentence longer than 14 words unless it is a deliberate rhetorical device.
- [ ] Contractions used naturally (you’re, don’t, it’s, we’ll).
- [ ] Zero corporate jargon (“leverage”, “optimize your financial journey”, “empower your future”).
- [ ] Sounds like a real person talking to a friend, not a script.
- [ ] Emotional honesty is preferred over politeness.
- [ ] Numbers are spoken the way people actually say them (“three lakh”, “ten thousand a month”).

## Quick rewrite patterns
- “It is important to build an emergency fund” → “Build the emergency fund first. Period.”
- “Many individuals struggle with…” → “Most of us have been there…”
- “Consider the following strategies” → “Here’s what actually works.”

If the line still feels robotic after one rewrite, delete it and write the meaning in plain speech from scratch.
